description: Verify content and design follows brand guidelines
---

Review content and components for brand compliance:

1. Ask me what to review (specific file, component, or content)

2. Check against brand guidelines (`.claude/brand-guidelines.md`):

   **Voice & Tone:**
   - Confident but not arrogant
   - Human, not corporate
   - Helpful, not pushy
   - Professional, not stuffy
   - Appropriate for context (homepage vs support)

   **Messaging:**
   - Benefit-driven, not just feature lists
   - Uses active voice
   - Avoids jargon and clichés
   - Includes real-world examples
   - Clear value proposition

   **Visual Design:**
   - Color usage correct (Charcoal #121212, Vermilion #FF5A33, Teal #00C4B3, Sand #E9E7E5)
   - Typography follows scale and hierarchy
   - Proper spacing and layout
   - Component styling consistent with design system
   - Dark/light mode support

   **Content Guidelines:**
   - Headlines under 60 characters
   - Paragraphs 2-3 sentences max
   - Strong CTAs ("Get Started for Free" not "Click Here")
   - Numbers formatted correctly
   - Second person voice ("you," "your")

   **Accessibility:**
   - Color contrast meets WCAG AA (4.5:1)
   - Touch targets minimum 44x44px
   - Alternative text for images
   - Keyboard accessible

3. Provide:
   - Brand compliance score
   - Specific issues with examples
   - Recommendations for improvement
   - Suggested rewrites for problematic content
   - Code fixes for design issues

4. Highlight any strong examples that exemplify the brand well

The goal is to maintain consistent brand experience across all touchpoints.
